Freight & Carrier Fraud Risk Taxonomy

An open, structured reference for fraud and cargo-loss patterns in European road freight. Every pattern lists the indicators that actually reveal it, where each one is observable, the false positives that make it easy to get wrong, and the countermeasures that address it.

Source, schema and machine-readable data: github.com/Jeevan-0508/freight-fraud-taxonomy
Compiled from public industry, law-enforcement and regulatory sources. Contains no confidential or employer-specific material. Provided for risk-assessment purposes; it is not legal advice.
